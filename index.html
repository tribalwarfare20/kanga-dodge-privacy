Kanga Dodge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Kanga Dodge handles information when you play the game.

2. Information and analytics

Kanga Dodge does not require players to create an account or directly submit personal information. The game uses analytics to understand gameplay, improve performance and identify technical problems. Depending on the analytics service integrated, information may include gameplay events, session duration, crash reports, app version, device type, operating system, IP address or device identifiers.

3. How information is used

Analytics information may be used to understand gameplay, improve the game, diagnose crashes and maintain security and reliability.

4. Third-party services

Analytics information may be processed by third-party service providers. The provider's identity and privacy policy should be added here after confirming which analytics service the game uses.

5. Advertising

Kanga Dodge does not display advertisements in its current design.

6. Sharing and retention

Analytics information may be processed by service providers to operate and improve the game. Retention depends on the analytics service and its configuration.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, although no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

The game's audience and analytics settings should be reviewed to ensure compliance with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at this webpage.
